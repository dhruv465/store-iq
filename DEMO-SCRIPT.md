# Demo video script — 5 minutes, the TV scenario

Record the phone screen (WhatsApp, the Store IQ number) with a voice-over. One take per beat, cut
together. Send `reset` before recording so the customer starts empty. Replies are written live from the
catalogue, so read what comes back; do not script the words Store IQ says.

The customer: a family moving into a new flat. Living room, they sit about three metres back, father
watches cricket, the room gets afternoon sun, budget around Rs 60,000. They think "QLED and OLED are the
same thing".

| Time      | What you send                                                                                                                                           | What to point at on screen                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:20 | — (voice-over)                                                                                                                                          | The visits: median 6 of 14, price first in 6 of 6, two questions before the first product. Store IQ keeps the associate in our best associate's order.                                       |
| 0:20–0:45 | `brief`                                                                                                                                                 | **Before the shift.** Televisions in stock with prices and today's offers, from the live catalogue. Say plainly: the data has no video or quiz, so none is shown.                            |
| 0:45–1:25 | 🎤 Voice note: "Family, new flat, living room, they sit about three metres back, father watches cricket, lots of afternoon sun, around sixty thousand." | **Assess.** Acknowledged at once. The needs filed on the card, and one `Ask:` question that would change what is shown. No price yet.                                                        |
| 1:25–2:00 | Answer the question, then type: "customer says QLED and OLED are the same"                                                                              | **Validate.** A line that confirms the true part, then corrects.                                                                                                                             |
| 2:00–2:40 | 📷 Photo of a TV shelf tag                                                                                                                              | **Translate.** The printed spec turned into a line for this home. The figures come from the catalogue, never from the photo.                                                                 |
| 2:40–3:15 | "show me options"                                                                                                                                       | **Recommend + demonstrate.** Three TVs from the store, each with a reason that starts with the customer's own words ("cricket: …") and what to show. Three lines, three buttons.             |
| 3:15–3:45 | Tap a TV, then **+ Wall mount**                                                                                                                         | **Complete.** The itemised total adds up in code, with the brand's real mount at its real price.                                                                                             |
| 3:45–4:10 | "any offers or EMI on this?"                                                                                                                            | **Close.** The gate is open now (needs known, difference shown): the real offer and finance in one sentence, or "none is running on it today".                                               |
| 4:10–4:30 | Tap **They agreed**                                                                                                                                     | **Follow through.** Summary for the customer only after the tap, a task with an owner, a check-back. Say plainly: the catalogue lists no installer site needs, so no site check is invented. |
| 4:30–4:45 | `review`                                                                                                                                                | **Review.** The visits' 0–14 card from what this conversation proved, and one thing to try next time.                                                                                        |
| 4:45–5:00 | Hindi voice note, then `manager`                                                                                                                        | **Extra points.** Hindi in, Hindi out; Marathi works too. The manager view; mention Ask an expert and `visit` in one breath.                                                                 |

## Before recording

- Number up: `npm run dev` and `npm run tunnel` running, Mac awake.
- Do one dry run with this script. A text reply takes 3–5 s and a voice note or photo 7–8 s; cut the waits.
- Photograph a real shelf tag; do not use a rendered one in the video.
- Use only the owner's phone and the test number. No real customer's name, number or face on screen.
