## 6. Functional requirements for the demo

| #   | Requirement                                                          | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Receive and process associate voice notes                            | **Done.** Uploaded to fal storage and transcribed by `fal-ai/elevenlabs/speech-to-text` (Whisper dropped clear notes and misheard Hindi). The words are then read exactly as typed words. Walked with the real voice notes from the test chat in English and Hindi; acknowledged at once, answered in 7–8 s.                               |
| 2   | Create, display and correct a home card                              | **Done, only where it helps** (decided 2 Nov). Needs are filed as the customer states them and read back with a correction button on `card`. Each new customer starts an empty card, with no inherited pick or consent.                                                                                                                    |
| 3   | Suggest the next question based on the current card                  | **Done, by the model.** Asked only if the answer changes what is shown. No question is written in code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 4   | Accept a product-label image, return a customer-friendly explanation | **Done, on fal.** `openrouter/router/vision` reads the tag's printed text; that text is treated as if the associate had said it, so the reply is written from the live catalogue and a misread tag cannot become a price. Walked with a rendered shelf tag; **not yet with a phone photo of a real tag.**                                  |
| 5   | Three recommendations, one reason **and one demonstration cue** each | **Done, model-written.** Three options from the live rows. The reason is structural: the model names the customer's need (`because`) and what it means (`reason`, "a fast-moving ball is the thing to show"); the code prints the customer's own words first and drops a reason it cannot tie to them. A cue is asked for, not guaranteed. |
| 6   | Calculate and display an itemised total from supplied data           | **Done, in code.** `itemisedTotal()` adds the pick and any tapped line. A TV pick is offered the brand's real wall mount at its real catalogue price, unless the TV's own offers already include one. Net payable and the saving off MRP are shown behind the offer gate.                                                                  |
| 7   | Gate offers until assessment and demonstration are complete          | **Done, and opens when the associate asks outright** (decided 2 Nov). `offerLeaked()` drops a line that volunteers one. "Offers on these" answers for the products on screen, not a fresh search.                                                                                                                                          |
| 8   | Explain finance using only supplied terms and full cost              | **Done, from the store's own terms.** A row's `pg_offer` gives months, instalment, total and saving, all in FACTS and checked by the figure guard.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 9   | Consent-based summary and follow-up task                             | **Done, in code.** Selection comes from a tap only; the summary waits for the "They agreed" tap, which is put first while a pick waits. Consent with no pick asks which product. Changing the product withdraws consent, and Store IQ never claims to be sending anything.                                                                 |
| 10  | Conversation score and one improvement suggestion                    | **Done, in code.** The store visits' card: seven steps, 0–2 each, Guide at 11, scored only from what the session can prove (a read photo is Translate, a reported doubt is Validate, a tied reason with products shown is Demonstrate). A yes to nothing scores nothing.                                                                   |

## Floor rules (contest §2)

| Rule                                | State                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First reply in under 5 seconds      | **Usually.** Taps and commands answer in milliseconds. A text turn is two model requests plus the store: 3–5 s. A request that hangs is asked again at 4.5 s and the first answer wins, instead of waiting out the 9 s timeout. Voice and photo are acknowledged at once and answered in 7–8 s. |
| Not more than 3 lines and 3 buttons | **Holds, enforced on every reply** by `floor()` at the exit; a test walks a whole conversation in three languages.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| A reason in each answer             | **Back, when it is the customer's.** See requirement 5. A reason that only narrates the conversation is not shown.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Voice and photo first               | **Both on fal, one key.** Voice proven with real notes; photo with a rendered tag.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## 7. Guardrails

| Guardrail                                                                   | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Test data and a test WhatsApp number only                                   | **Data holds:** the live catalogue test access given to us, reads only; `catalog.ts` can GET an allowlist of paths and nothing else. `data/pack.json` is used only with no credentials. **Number open:** it is the owner's own; whether to switch to a test number is the owner's decision. |
| Never message a real customer                                               | Holds. Replies go only to numbers that wrote first, inside Meta's 24-hour window. Testing uses `POST /try`, which queues nothing.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Never invent a price, offer, stock, specification, finance term or discount | Holds. Figures checked as unit pairs in English, Hindi and Marathi; one retry, then withheld. Installation, delivery and mounts are never "free" or "included" unless the data says so.                                                                                                     |
| Never infer wealth from appearance                                          | Holds. `start_level` is only written from what the associate supplies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Keep the associate in control                                               | Holds. Every reply is a line to say or an `Ask:` question; names products, never item codes.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Show uncertainty when data is missing                                       | Holds. A search that misses or a store that does not answer is told to the model as that, and offered to a named expert.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Home card is interaction-scoped                                             | Holds. A new customer clears the card, the pick, consent and the site check.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Extra points (contest §2)

| Item                              | State                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hindi and another Indian language | **Done: Hindi and Marathi, plus English.** Every code reply and button in all three; model replies follow the language just used. Typed Devanagari is told apart by Marathi and Hindi grammar words.                                                           |
| Department manager view           | **Done, two ways.** `manager` (or `team`) on WhatsApp; `/manager` portal with each associate's seven step marks, the live floor, objections and products moving, and a broadcast of today's offers that holds anyone outside the window until they next write. |
| Ask an expert                     | **Done.** "Find out for me" routes by subject to a named owner with a reference and a line to hold the customer.                                                                                                                                               |
| Store visit log by voice note     | **Done.** `visit`, then speak or type; filed against the store its words name, never read as a customer.                                                                                                                                                       |
